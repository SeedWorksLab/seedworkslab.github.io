Grape

A calmer way to keep up. Grape brings headlines from the publishers you choose into a paper-inspired daily edition, then lets you build a reading queue with just a few gestures.

Release status Read the documentation View source
Android firstEarly developmentAGPL-3.0
The idea

Keep up without keeping busy.

A focused daily news ritual rather than another endless feed.

Choose the sources

Start with trusted publisher feeds and decide which publications belong in your edition.

Build a reading queue

Skip a headline, save its publisher-supplied excerpt, or keep the link to the full article.

Know what is next

Followed stories, cited AI summaries and a verified breaking-news brief are planned, not yet available.
