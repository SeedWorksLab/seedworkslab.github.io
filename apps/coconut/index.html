Personal finance

Coconut

Coconut models one person's financial life rather than a bank or an accounting system. Every month you get a monthly allowance, plan fixed and flexible expenses against it, and any surplus flows automatically into a savings reserve.

Download Coconut Read the documentation View source

What it does

A budget that starts with what you have.

Coconut keeps the month in focus: one allowance, the bills you already know about, the flexible spending you choose, and savings that grow from what is left.

Allowance-first planning

Set the money available for the month, plan fixed costs and flexible budgets, and keep every past month intact.

Shared expenses, clearly split

Track a shared space with explicit per-person splits and generate a closing report when the period ends.

Private and offline by default

Your SQLite database stays on your device. Sync is optional, and self-hosting your own Supabase project is supported.
