Meal planning

Banana

Banana plans the week around the time and energy you actually have. It turns weekday time budgets, pantry stock and local market offers into a realistic menu and a shopping list you can follow.

Download Banana Read the documentation View source

What it does

Meals that fit the week you have.

No fantasy meal plans. Banana works with the real constraints: a busy Tuesday, food already in the cupboard, and what is actually on offer.

Time-aware weekly plans

Give each weekday a realistic cooking budget and let Banana balance quick meals, leftovers and no-cook days.

Deal-aware shopping

Local market offers from Lidl, Hofer and Spar feed the plan, while the shopping list stays organised by store aisle.

Pantry and preferences

Use what you already have, respect diets and allergies, and keep the weekly food budget in view.
