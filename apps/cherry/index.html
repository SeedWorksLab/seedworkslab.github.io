Coffee journal

Cherry

Cherry is a voice-first coffee journal for planning a brew, recording it hands-free, and actually tasting the result. A deterministic recommender learns from your history, while optional AI supplies narrative when you ask for it.

Download Cherry Read the documentation View source
Android Voice first Optional AI AGPL-3.0
What it does

A brew log you can talk to.

Keep the phone at arm's length, talk through the brew, and capture the details that make the next cup better.

Plan, brew, taste

Build a brew plan, follow it, and save voice notes with phase markers that stay linked to the brew timeline.

Transparent extraction

Every extracted field keeps its provenance and confidence. Review, edit or reject what the model suggested.

Private by design

Data stays on device first. Optional AI runs on your own key, and the recommender itself is deterministic and local.
