The family

Three apps. One way of working.

Each app is named after a fruit and shaped around one everyday routine. They share the same architecture, the same design language, and the same promise: your data stays yours.

Personal finance

Coconut

A budget app built around a monthly allowance and a savings reserve, not a bank account. Plan the month, track what you spend, and let the surplus move into savings.

  • Monthly allowance and savings reserve
  • Fixed, flexible and shared expenses
  • Optional sync through SeedWorks Cloud or your own Supabase

Coffee brewing

Cherry

A coffee journal that makes recording a brew easier than skipping it. Talk through the process, capture how it tasted, and get a concrete suggestion for the next cup.

  • Voice-first brew recording
  • Nine brewing methods and a bean catalogue
  • Deterministic next-brew recommendations

What we believe

Software should earn its place.

We keep the feature set small, the data close, and the promises simple. These principles shape every SeedWorks app.

01

Private by default

No mandatory account, no analytics, no advertising. Your data lives on your device unless you explicitly choose otherwise.

02

Offline first

Every app works fully without a connection. Manual entry is always as capable as any assisted path.

03

One job well

Each app does one thing, for one person, on one device. We add features that shorten the loop, not features that fill a grid.

04

Yours to keep

Export your database to a portable file whenever you want. No lock-in, no subscription gate around your own history.

Under the surface

A shared foundation, not a shared template.

The apps are separate products, but they are built from the same deliberate stack: a deterministic core, a local database, and a Material 3 interface that feels at home on every screen.
