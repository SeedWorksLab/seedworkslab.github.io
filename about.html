About SeedWorks

A small studio with a simple promise.

SeedWorks makes focused tools for everyday life. We believe software can be useful without being demanding, connected without being extractive, and smart without putting your data on someone else's server.

A seed is small, self-contained, and carries everything it needs to grow. That is the shape we want our software to take.
Independent Privacy first Open source Built in the open

SeedWorks started with a frustration: the apps that should make daily life easier often make it heavier. They ask for an account before they are useful, upload your history by default, and charge a subscription for access to your own data.

We wanted the opposite. Small apps that open quickly, work on a plane, and keep their records on the device they belong to. Apps that treat a budget, a coffee brew or a week of meals as something worth paying attention to, without turning that attention into a product.

The fruit names keep us honest. They are approachable, memorable and a little playful. They also remind us that each app is a single, self-contained thing, not a platform waiting to absorb everything else.

Today the family is Coconut for money, Cherry for coffee and Banana for food. More fruit is already being planted. The details will differ, but the promise will not: private by default, offline first, and yours to keep.

What we won't do

The line we draw.

A privacy promise is only meaningful if it rules things out. These are the things SeedWorks apps will not do.

No ads or trackers

No advertising, no analytics, no telemetry and no third-party profiling. Your attention is not the business model.

No account wall

You can use every core feature without registering. Optional sync is yours to configure, not a requirement for entry.

No data lock-in

Your records can be exported to a portable file at any time. The local database is the source of truth, not a service we control.

No AI dependency

Assisted features are optional. The decisions that shape your data are made by deterministic, testable logic, and manual entry is always equal.

How an app gets made

Start small. Prove the loop. Then grow.

Every SeedWorks app follows the same rough path from an ordinary annoyance to a tool you can rely on.

Start with a routine

We pick something people do often and usually do badly: tracking money, logging a brew, planning a week of meals. The app exists to shorten the distance between intention and action.

Make it local

The database is designed first. It lives on the device, uses integer money and explicit units, and can be exported whole. Nothing depends on a backend being available.

Build the deterministic core

Budgets, recommendations, plans and splits are calculated by pure service-layer logic with unit tests. The important decisions are predictable, inspectable and repeatable.

Add intelligence carefully

AI is optional and clearly bounded. It can transcribe, extract or narrate, but it never writes directly to the database and never makes a decision that affects your records.

Ship the whole loop

An app is ready when one full cycle works without a connection: plan, act, record and learn. Then we polish the interface, the backups and the edges.

A shared foundation

Different fruit, same roots.

The apps are independent products, but they are built from a common architecture. That keeps them consistent, testable and portable across Android, Linux and the web.
