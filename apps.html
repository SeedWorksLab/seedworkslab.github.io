The app family

One fruit for every routine.

Coconut, Cherry and Banana are separate tools built on one shared foundation. Each one is private by default, works offline, and is designed to turn a vague intention into a deliberate habit.

Coconut Cherry Banana

Coconut

Personal finance

Coconut models one person's financial life rather than a bank or an accounting system. Every month you get a monthly allowance, plan fixed and flexible expenses against it, and any surplus flows automatically into a savings reserve.

  • Monthly allowance with past months kept intact
  • Fixed expenses with four estimation strategies
  • Flexible budgets tracked against actual spending
  • One-off income and yearly subscriptions
  • Transactions with attachments and statistics
  • Shared expenses with per-person splits
  • Closing reports and settlement summaries
  • Optional multi-device sync via your own Supabase

Cherry

Coffee brewing

Cherry is a personal coffee journal, not a social network or a cloud service. You plan a brew, talk through it while you make it, record how it tasted, and the app gradually learns how the variables you control affect the result in the cup.

  • Plan, brew and tasted lifecycle in one record
  • Voice-first recording with phase markers
  • Cloud transcription and LLM extraction
  • Field provenance for every extracted value
  • Bean catalogue, brewers and grinders
  • Nine brewing methods with canonical recipes
  • Technique coaching and recipe exploration
  • Deterministic next-brew recommender

Banana

Meal planning

Banana plans your next week of meals around how much time you actually have and what you specifically want to eat, then turns the plan into one organised shopping list aware of current deals at Lidl, Hofer and Spar.

  • Plan, shop, cook and rate weekly loop
  • Per-weekday time budgets and no-cook days
  • Food wishes and batch cooking as first-class
  • Recipe library with ingredients and ratings
  • Pantry tracking that subtracts what you have
  • Diet, allergies, dislikes and weekly budget
  • Deal scraping for Lidl, Hofer and Spar
  • Aisle-grouped list with the best offer flagged
Android Deal aware AGPL-3.0
