What a Seed account does

  • Optional by design. Apps work offline and locally without signing in.
  • Only what you connect. The page lists usage that apps explicitly report.
  • No tracking scripts. The account page runs from SeedWorks-owned code only.

      Delete Seed account

      This permanently deletes your account and your personal hosted data. Shared Spaces you own transfer to another active member, or are deleted if no member remains. Data kept only on your devices is not touched. This cannot be undone.
