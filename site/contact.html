Before you post

Help us reproduce it.

A good issue is the fastest route to a fix. Include the app and version, what you expected, what happened instead, and the steps that get you there. If the problem involves data, describe the shape of it rather than pasting private records.

  • The app name, version and platform (Android, Linux or web)
  • Clear steps to reproduce the problem
  • What you expected and what actually happened
  • Any error message, log or screenshot that helps
  • Whether the app was online or offline at the time

Please never include API keys, Supabase credentials, financial records or other private data in a public issue.
